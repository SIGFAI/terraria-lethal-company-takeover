# Lethal Company Takeover

**Terraria** × Lethal Company · visual · idea by the team

Bring Lethal Company into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I dragged Lethal Company into Terraria: Hoarding Bugs, a Bracken, and a Coil-Head that freezes when you look at it. The Stop Sign is a great weapon.

## Install

Copy Sigf.tmod into Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods, then enable "SIGF Mix" in tModLoader's Workshop > Manage Mods menu and start a world.
